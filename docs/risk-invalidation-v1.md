# Risk and invalidation references v1

Technical scan candidates now include screen-risk flags and a moving-average monitoring reference (20-day where available, otherwise 50-day). Flags include below-average price, negative 20-session momentum, a daily decline of at least 3%, and missing volume comparison.

These are monitoring references only—not stop-loss orders, individualized risk levels, or advice. Moving averages lag and do not account for volatility, liquidity, gaps, event risk, sizing or user constraints. No strategy backtest has been performed.
