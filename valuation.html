Market Mind research tool · Not personal financial advice. DCF is sensitive to long-run assumptions. For miners use mid-cycle commodity prices and through-cycle capex; for property use recurring operating earnings, NAV and cap rates; for turnaround businesses normalise earnings carefully; for platform businesses stress-test reinvestment and incremental returns on capital. Verify accounting definitions, leases, pensions, minority interests, convertibles and share dilution before relying on per-share outputs.
