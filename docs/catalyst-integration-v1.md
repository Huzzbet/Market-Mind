# Catalyst integration v1

- News items that include matchedSymbols are associated with scanner candidates using the exact ticker key.
- Up to three headlines are attached to technical leader records; up to two headline items are shown in catalyst signal cards.
- Catalyst cards are separately scored for research prioritisation, not added to technical scores. The score is a simple visibility ranking based on matched headline count, not probability, impact, or recommendation.
- Headlines and classifications are heuristic. Verify the original source and publication time before treating any item as a catalyst.
- No backtest or runtime integration test has been performed.
